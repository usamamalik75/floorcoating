Project: Unified Service Operations Platform

1. Introduction

The purpose of this SRS is to describe the system that will be designed and presented for client review. It is intended to confirm that the planned workflow, modules, screens, terminology, and business rules align with the client's operational requirements.

Important: This document presents the proposed solution in future-state terms. It should be reviewed as a scope and requirement reference for what the platform will provide.

2. Business Goal and Product Scope

Primary Goal

The platform will provide one connected system where a customer record can move from enquiry through qualification, assessment, estimate, proposal, scheduling, field work, invoicing, and payment tracking.

Business Problem Being Solved

The solution will reduce fragmented handoffs between CRM tools, estimating workflows, spreadsheets, dispatch coordination, and accounting follow-up by consolidating them into one operational product.

Target Use Case

The system will support quote-driven service businesses managing structured work such as commercial, industrial, and project-based jobs that require assessments, proposals, teams, materials, scheduling, and customer approval.

Delivery Intent

The initial interactive phase will be used to validate workflow coverage and business fit, after which backend, live integrations, and production infrastructure may be planned separately.

3. User Roles

Role Purpose in System
Administrator Will configure standards, fields, templates, workflows, and company-level views.
Owner Will review operational performance, open pipeline, and overall business status.
Sales Representative Will own lead follow-up, qualification, visit scheduling, proposal follow-up, and customer communication.
Estimator Will build estimates, review site data, select catalogue items, and approve proposal readiness.
Project Manager Will own awarded work, scheduling readiness, issue escalation, and delivery oversight.
Crew Leader / Technician Will execute field work, checklists, photos, issues, progress logging, and on-site customer coordination.
Accounting Will raise invoices, track balances, issue payment links, and monitor collections.
Customer Will review proposals, sign scope, complete sign-off, and pay through hosted payment experiences.

4. Target End-to-End Workflow

  1. A lead will be captured and routed to the correct territory or location.
  2. The lead will be qualified and assigned to a sales owner.
  3. A site visit or sales call will be scheduled and completed using guided forms.
  4. An estimate will be built from site data and configured company standards.
  5. A proposal will be reviewed, approved, and sent to the customer.
  6. The customer will review options, sign electronically, and proceed to deposit or payment steps.
  7. The job will be scheduled, staffed, and tracked operationally.
  8. The field team will record progress, issues, checklists, and completion evidence.
  9. Accounting will raise invoices and monitor hosted payment requests.
  10. The customer will complete sign-off and payment tracking will be visible internally.

FR-007 The system shall provide a communication workspace for follow-ups and customer messages. Planned for interactive phase The communications workflow will include inbox, threads, drafts, send actions, and opportunity-level messaging views.
FR-008 The system shall allow proposal send workflows from the estimate builder. Planned for interactive phase Users will be able to prepare a proposal message flow with timeline visibility and delivery options.

FR-012 The system shall track materials and purchasing against awarded work. Planned in scope The system will support material requirement views, purchasing status, and order progression.
FR-013 The system shall support customer completion sign-off. Planned in scope The customer journey will include completion sign-off and related change-order acknowledgement.
FR-014 The system shall provide invoicing and payment tracking views for accounting. Planned in scope Accounting users will be able to review billed/received status, raise invoices, and track balances.
FR-015 The system shall provide hosted payment request experiences for customers. Planned for interactive phase The customer payment journey will include hosted payment-link screens and internal status tracking.
FR-016 The system shall show communications and payment progress back on the opportunity record. Planned in scope The opportunity record will surface customer thread history and payment-link status.

7. Reporting Requirements

  • The system shall display role-based dashboards for operational visibility.
  • The system shall display sales, job, finance, and location performance summaries.
  • The system shall show follow-ups due, open proposals, and communication queues.
  • The system shall show invoice and payment status across the business.
Initial reporting will focus on workflow visibility and management summaries. Advanced analytics, forecasting, and production business intelligence may be handled in later phases.

10. Client Review and Sign-Off Guidance

The client should review this specification against the following questions:

  1. Does the proposed workflow match the intended business process from lead through payment?
  2. Are all major modules represented in the correct sequence and ownership model?
  3. Does the terminology align with the client's business vocabulary?
  4. Do the planned customer-facing and internal screens reflect expected behavior?
  5. Do the planned admin builders reflect the required level of configurability?
  6. Are any required modules, fields, screens, or handoffs missing from scope?
Sign-Off Statement: If the client confirms that the workflow, modules, and screen expectations described in this document reflect the intended business requirements, then this specification may be treated as the approved scope for the interactive delivery phase.
